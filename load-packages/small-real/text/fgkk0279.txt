INSYS Therapeutics, Inc. Consultant Meetings for SUBSYS®

Objective:
Gain insight and consultation on the perception of SUBSYS and the motivation to prescribe SUBSYS
when comparing a cohort of early adopters and a cohort of those who have limited or no experience
with the product. Questions that need to be answered:
•
•
•
•
•
•

Are there overall perception differences between the groups and what is driving them?
Are there environmental differences (demographic, coverage, etc)?
Has extent of exposure to SUBSYS clinical information or Savings Program contributed to
differences in prescribing?
What was (were) the driving factor(s) to begin prescribing SUBSYS?
Are there specific barriers that were overcome for some and not others?
Do the non-prescribers have objections or obstacles that are unique to them that have not been
addressed through the existing marketing programs and communications?

Rationale:
Since the launch of SUBSY, the growth of the product has been heavily stratified between early adopters
and those who have limited or no experience. Though the growth of the number of new prescribers has
been steadily increasing, the growth in overall prescriptions remains somewhat dependent on the
growth within a given prescribers practice. To achieve a more sustainable growth trend, an additional
effort is being initiated to increase the number of prescribers within the target list. This consultant
meeting is being executed to better understand what prompted the change to begin using SUBSYS for
those who are prescribing. Additionally, our goal is to identify how to increase the impact of what has
been shown to be successful. Equally, we hope to gain insight into what communications, channels, or
strategies may be useful going forward in removing obstacles that remain for those who have not
prescribed.
