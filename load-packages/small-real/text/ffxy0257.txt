3. Indication 
 
Dronabinol Oral Solution is an orally active cannabinoid which, like other 
cannabinoids, has complex effects on the central nervous system (CNS) including 
central sympathomimetic activity indicated for the treatment of nausea and vomiting 
associated with cancer chemotherapy in patients who have failed to respond adequately 
to conventional antiemetic treatments and anorexia associated with weight loss in 
patients with AIDS.

8. A draft list of specific questions, grouped by discipline 
 
Abuse Potential 
 
Insys acknowledges that Dronabinol Oral Solution is currently classified as a Schedule 
I drug under the Controlled Substances Act (CSA) and that upon FDA approval of the 
NDA, the drug product will be placed by DEA into a different schedule of the CSA 
based on the characterization of the formulation’s abuse potential relative to Marinol.  
We agree that the ability to schedule a product is critical to product labeling.   
 
In order to substantiate the abuse potential of the Insys product as compared to 
Marinol, Insys evaluated the physical properties, potential tampering, and altered uses 
of the to-be-marketed Dronabinol Oral Solution including smoking and intravenous 
injection using Marinol as a positive control.   
 
Additionally, Insys conducted an Eight Factor Analysis of the abuse potential for the 
proposed Dronabinol Oral Solution formulation as supplementary information for the 
Agency’s consideration. 
 
In response to the FDA e-mail communication received from Mary Chung dated 
August 8, 2013, Insys plans to conduct and subsequently submit a clinical human abuse 
potential study to confirm the abuse potential/liability of Dronabinol Oral Solution. 
 
Insys respectfully disagrees that the absence of clinical abuse study data in the initial 
sequence of the NDA renders the application incomplete for review.  According to the 
regulations, the NDA is considered sufficiently complete to warrant substantive review 
under the following provisions: 
 
? The NDA contains a description and analysis of studies related to the abuse of 
the drug, including a proposal for scheduling under the Controlled Substances 
Act in accordance with 21 CFR 314.50(d)(5)(vii).
